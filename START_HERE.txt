ChokePilot -- Start Here
=========================

Two files to open:

1. docs/report.pdf
   The full technical report: process/model identification, controller
   design, and results for Scenarios A/B/C/D with plots, seed-sweep safety
   distributions, and baseline comparisons.

2. docs/ChokePilot_presentation_template.pdf
   The presentation slides (Honeywell SIH submission template, filled in).

Everything else in this archive is supporting source code, data, and
generated outputs -- see README.md for the full repo layout and how to
reproduce every number in the report/presentation from scratch.
